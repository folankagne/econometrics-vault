title: Panel Data Methods — Overview
---
Neither course covers general panel-data estimation in its own right — Econ 2b's differencing material ([cross-section and before-after estimators](../difference-in-differences/cross-section-and-before-after-estimators.md), [standard DiD](../difference-in-differences/standard-difference-in-differences.md)) is really panel data with exactly two periods, used for causal identification. This folder generalizes that machinery to panels with any number of periods and states it as a topic in its own right: pooling versus genuine panel data, the unobserved-effects model, the fixed-effects (within) and random-effects estimators, and the choice between them. It draws entirely on material beyond both courses — chiefly Wooldridge (2016), Ch.13–14 — and every entry is tagged `beyond-lectures` accordingly.

## Reading order

1. [Pooled Cross Sections and the Unobserved Effects Model](pooled-cross-sections-and-the-unobserved-effects-model.md)
2. [The First-Differenced Estimator, Revisited for T > 2](the-first-differenced-estimator.md)
3. [The Fixed Effects (Within) Estimator](fixed-effects-within-estimator.md)
4. [Fixed Effects versus First Differencing](fixed-effects-vs-first-differencing.md)
5. [The Random Effects Model and GLS](random-effects-model-and-gls.md)
6. [Fixed versus Random Effects, and the Hausman Test](fixed-vs-random-effects-and-the-hausman-test.md)
7. [Unbalanced Panels and Clustered Standard Errors](unbalanced-panels-and-clustered-standard-errors.md)

See also: [two-way fixed effects](../difference-in-differences/two-way-fixed-effects.md), which is the special case of the fixed-effects estimator with a binary treatment and group/time dummies — this folder develops the general panel-data machinery that TWFE is one application of.
