title: Generalized Method of Moments — Overview
---
GMM is the unifying estimation framework behind almost every estimator already developed in this vault — OLS, IV, and (asymptotically) MLE are all special cases — but neither course states it as a general method in its own right. This short folder makes that unification explicit, tagged `beyond-lectures` throughout and sourced to Hansen's (1982) foundational paper and Wooldridge's graduate treatment (2010).

## Reading order

1. [Moment Conditions and the Method of Moments](moment-conditions-and-the-method-of-moments.md)
2. [The GMM Estimator and Efficient Weighting](the-gmm-estimator-and-efficient-weighting.md)
3. [GMM Nests OLS, IV, and MLE](gmm-nests-ols-iv-and-mle.md)
4. [Overidentification and the J-Test](overidentification-and-the-j-test.md)

See also: [the analogy principle](../causal-inference-foundations/parameter-estimand-and-estimator.md), the vault's earlier, informal introduction to exactly the same idea — replacing population moment conditions with their sample analogs — that this folder now generalizes and formalizes.
